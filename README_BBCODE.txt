[SIZE="5"][COLOR="SeaGreen"]One APH a Time[/COLOR][/SIZE]
[COLOR="Gray"][i]Withdraw on a stack of recipes, motifs, fragments, containers or furnishings takes one; Get All takes one of everything the settings allow. Learned or opened items are used when you close the storage; furnishings just stay in your backpack.[/i][/COLOR]

[SIZE="3"][COLOR="DarkOrchid"]Dependencies:[/COLOR][/SIZE]

This addon has no required dependencies.

Optionals for additional features:
[LIST]
[*] [url="https://www.esoui.com/downloads/info4917-LibAPH.html"][COLOR="#FF69B4"]LibAPH[/COLOR][/url] [COLOR="Gray"][i](Cleans up the memory left after your stored items are used, a little each frame so the game never stutters)[/i][/COLOR]
[*] [url="https://www.esoui.com/downloads/info7-LibAddonMenu-2.0.html"][COLOR="#FF69B4"]LibAddonMenu-2.0[/COLOR][/url] [COLOR="Gray"][i](PC Settings Menu)[/i][/COLOR]
[*] [url="https://www.esoui.com/downloads/info584-LibHarvensAddonSettings.html"][COLOR="#FF69B4"]LibHarvensAddonSettings[/COLOR][/url] [COLOR="Gray"][i](Console Settings Menu)[/i][/COLOR]
[/LIST]

[b]Without the optional Dependencies:[/b] You can still run the addon entirely independent, and control its settings via built-in slash commands as a standalone utility.

[SIZE="3"][COLOR="DarkOrchid"]Slash Commands[/COLOR][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*] [color=#00FFFF]/oneaphatime[/color]: turns the add-on on or off (so does the Turn One APH a Time On or Off key, Alt+D by default, shown next to Get All at a storage); while it is off, withdrawing takes the whole stack.
[*] [color=#00FFFF]/oneaphatime chat[/color]: shows or hides One APH a Time's messages in chat (the same as the Chat Logs toggle in the settings).
[/LIST]
